# Marketing Campaign Performance: A/B Testing, Regression & Time-Series Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project evaluates the full-year performance of two parallel digital advertising campaigns—**Facebook Ads** and **Google AdWords**—conducted throughout 2019 (365 days). Acting as a data analyst for a marketing agency, the objective is to identify which platform delivers superior return on investment (ROI) in terms of clicks, conversions, and cost-effectiveness, forecast expected conversions, and provide actionable budget allocation recommendations to stakeholders.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem & Research Questions
1. **Platform Effectiveness (A/B Testing):** Which advertising platform (Facebook Ads vs. Google AdWords) generates statistically significantly higher conversions and better cost efficiency?
2. **Click-to-Sale Efficiency (Correlation):** Do higher ad clicks strongly translate into actual sales conversions across both platforms?
3. **Conversion Forecasting (Regression):** Given a target number of clicks on the winning platform, how many conversions can the agency reliably expect?
4. **Temporal Optimization (Time-Series & Cointegration):** Which days of the week and months of the year yield peak conversions and the lowest Cost Per Conversion (CPC), and does ad spend share a long-term equilibrium with conversions?

---

## 📂 Dataset Description
* **Source:** Marketing Campaign Dataset (365 daily records from January 1, 2019, to December 31, 2019; 17 features).
* **Key Metrics Tracked (for both Facebook and AdWords):**
  * `Ad Views`: Daily impressions generated by the ad.
  * `Ad Clicks`: Daily clicks recorded on the ad.
  * `Ad Conversions`: Daily completed sales/desired actions.
  * `Cost per Ad`: Total daily advertising spend ($).
  * `Click-Through Rate (CTR)`: Ratio of clicks to views (%).
  * `Conversion Rate`: Ratio of conversions to clicks (%).
  * `Cost per Click (CPC)`: Average spend per click ($).

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Methodology
* **Language:** Python 3
* **Data Manipulation & Cleaning:** `pandas`, `numpy` (datetime parsing, currency/percentage string stripping, and float type conversion)

  * `statsmodels`: **Engle-Granger Cointegration Test** (`coint`) and seasonal decomposition for long-term spend-to-conversion equilibrium.

## 📊 Key Analytical Findings

### 1. Exploratory & Bucketed Conversion Performance
* **Facebook Ads** consistently achieved high daily conversion volumes:
  * **10–15 conversions/day:** 189 days
  * **6–10 conversions/day:** 128 days
  * **>15 conversions/day:** 47 days
  * **<6 conversions/day:** Only 1 day out of 365
* **Google AdWords** never exceeded 10 conversions in a single day across the entire year (0 days in `10–15` or `>15` buckets), remaining strictly in the `<6` and `6–10` conversion tiers.

### 2. Click-to-Conversion Correlation
* **Facebook Ads:** Strong positive linear correlation (**$r = 0.87$**) between clicks and conversions, indicating high-intent traffic.
* **Google AdWords:** Moderate positive correlation (**$r = 0.45$**), showing that increased click volume on AdWords frequently fails to translate into proportional sales.

### 3. A/B Hypothesis Testing (Two-Sample T-Test)
* **Mean Daily Conversions:** Facebook Ads (**11.7**) vs. Google AdWords (**~5.0**).
* **Test Results:** **$t\text{-statistic} = 32.8$**, **$p\text{-value} < 0.001$** ($9.3 \times 10^{-111}$).
* **Conclusion:** We reject the null hypothesis. Facebook Ads outperforms Google AdWords with overwhelming statistical significance.

### 4. Predictive Regression Modeling (Facebook Ads)
* **Model Performance:** **$R^2 = 76\%$**, **$\text{MSE} = 2.02$**.
* **Conversion Benchmarks:**
  * **50 daily clicks** $\rightarrow$ **~13 expected conversions**
  * **80 daily clicks** $\rightarrow$ **~19 expected conversions**

### 5. Time-Series & Long-Term Equilibrium
* **Weekly Pattern:** Conversions peak at the start of the workweek (**Mondays and Tuesdays**) and dip toward Fridays.
* **Monthly Trend:** Overall upward trajectory across 2019, though notable conversion dips occurred in **February, April, May, June, August, and November**.
* **Cost Efficiency:** Monthly Cost Per Conversion (CPC) remained stable between **$7.00 and $8.40**, with peak cost-efficiency (lowest CPC) in **May and November** and highest acquisition costs in **February**.
* **Cointegration Test:** Confirmed a statistically significant long-term equilibrium relationship ($p < 0.05$) between advertising spend (`Cost`) and `Conversions`.

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations
1. **Reallocate Ad Spend to Facebook:** Shift the majority of the advertising budget from Google AdWords to Facebook Ads to capitalize on its 2.3x higher average daily conversion rate and strong click-to-sale efficiency.
2. **Audit AdWords Targeting & Landing Pages:** Investigate why AdWords generates high views and moderate clicks without closing sales ($r = 0.45$).
3. **Front-Load Weekly Budgets:** Increase bid caps and launch promotional offers on **Mondays and Tuesdays** when user conversion intent is highest.
4. **Use Regression for Campaign Planning:** Leverage the linear regression model ($R^2 = 76\%$) to set realistic click targets for desired client sales quotas.
